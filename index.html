Collection 01 / Manifesto

Beauty
under restraint.

THE CAGE begins with the architecture that shapes a human before she chooses her own form.

Invisible systems become visible structure. Tailoring behaves like discipline. Transparent frameworks hold, frame and restrict the body—while light keeps passing through. The collection does not romanticize captivity; it turns constraint into an object we can finally see, question, reshape and eventually leave behind.
